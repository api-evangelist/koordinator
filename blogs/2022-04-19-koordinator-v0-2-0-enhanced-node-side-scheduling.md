We’re pleased to announce the release of Koordinator v0.2.0. Overview ​ Koordinator v0.1.0 implements basic co-location scheduling capabilities, and after the project was released, it has received attention and positive responses from the community. For some issues that everyone cares about, such as how to isolate resources for best-effort workloads, how to ensure the runtime stability of latency-sensitiv applications in co-location scenarios, etc., we have enhanced node-side scheduling capabilities in koordinator v0.2.0 to solve these problems.
